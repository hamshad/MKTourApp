01 · Introduction & Overview

Introduction & Project Overview

This Statement of Work sets out the terms, scope, deliverables, timeline, and investment for the development of the MK Food Delivery Platform between Moksha Solutions (the Developer) and MK-Tours (the Client). It defines the work to be undertaken, the deliverables the Client will receive, the responsibilities of both parties, and the commercial terms governing the engagement.

This SoW is to be read in conjunction with any accompanying Master Services Agreement (MSA) between the parties. In the event of a conflict, the MSA takes precedence.

Project Overview

The Client requires the development of a comprehensive food delivery platform serving the Milton Keynes area, United Kingdom. The platform consists of three interconnected components, each targeting a distinct user group:

ComponentTarget UsersDescription
Customer & Delivery Partner AppConsumers & Delivery DriversCross-platform mobile app — customers discover restaurants, place orders, and track deliveries. Delivery partners receive and complete deliveries within the same app.
Restaurant AppRestaurant Owners & StaffDedicated cross-platform mobile app for restaurant partners to manage menus, process orders, and monitor sales.
Admin Panel DashboardPlatform AdministratorsWeb-based management portal for user management, restaurant approvals, live order monitoring, and financial reporting.

All three components communicate with a single centralized backend API and shared database infrastructure. The project targets launch within 4 months of commencement, across iOS, Android, and web.

02 · Scope of Work

Scope of Work & Deliverables

The Developer will design and build a secure, scalable RESTful API and real-time event system, three client applications, and a web-based admin dashboard. This section defines every feature area and functional deliverable across all components.

Backend API

The backend API serves as the single source of truth for all three client applications. Key deliverables include:

  • Database schema design covering users, drivers, restaurants, menus, orders, and payments
  • RESTful API endpoints for all platform features
  • Real-time event infrastructure using WebSockets for live order status and delivery tracking
  • Secure authentication via phone-number-based OTP login
  • Payment processing integration supporting card payments and cash-on-delivery
  • Push notification service for all order lifecycle events
  • Geospatial querying to surface nearby restaurants based on user location
  • Virtual Ledger financial model — customer payments collected into the Client's account, with automated calculation of amounts owed to restaurants and delivery partners, net of platform commission
  • Document management system for delivery partner verification files
  • Server deployment on the Client's existing Hostinger KVM 2 infrastructure

Assumptions & Constraints

This SoW is based on the following assumptions. If any proves incorrect, scope, timeline, or cost may require formal revision.

  2. Platform scoped for Milton Keynes area only; multi-city expansion out of scope

  4. Existing Hostinger KVM 2 VPS has sufficient remaining capacity for launch-phase traffic
  5. Client or nominated contact available for feedback and approvals within 48 hours of request
  6. Developer not responsible for third-party API downtime, pricing changes, or policy changes
  7. App Store and Play Store review processes controlled by Apple and Google; Developer will prepare compliant submissions but cannot guarantee approval timelines
Delays in Client-provided items beyond the noted deadlines may shift the project timeline. The Developer cannot be held liable for resulting delays.
05 · Legal Terms

Legal Terms & Sign-Off

This section covers exclusions, acceptance criteria, intellectual property, confidentiality, change control, and the formal sign-off process. These terms govern the working relationship between Moksha Solutions and MK-Tours throughout the engagement.

Exclusions — Out of Scope

The following items are explicitly not included in this SoW. Any request to include them will be treated as a change request and quoted separately.

  • Promo codes, vouchers, or discount functionality
  • In-app chat between customers and delivery partners
  • Multi-order batching (concurrent orders to a single driver)
  • Loyalty/rewards points system
  • Scheduled or pre-booked orders
  • Automated payouts via Stripe Connect / escrow model
  • Alcohol delivery age-verification flow
  • Multi-language or localisation support
  • Multi-city or multi-region expansion
  • Third-party food aggregator integrations (Just Eat, Uber Eats, etc.)
  • Custom analytics or BI dashboards beyond Section 3.4 specifications
  • Copywriting, legal drafting, or content creation of any kind
  • Post-launch support and maintenance beyond 30-day defect warranty
  • Marketing, SEO, or social media services

Acceptance Criteria

Each deliverable is considered accepted when: the Developer notifies the Client of completion, the Client reviews and confirms in writing that requirements are met, or 7 calendar days pass with no written objection. The Developer provides a 30-day defect warranty from final deployment, covering bugs and defects only — not new features or scope changes.

Intellectual Property

Upon full payment of all milestones, full ownership of the platform's source code and deliverables transfers to the Client. The Developer retains the right to reference the project in portfolio and case studies unless the Client requests otherwise in writing. Third-party libraries remain subject to their respective open-source licences. Third-party service accounts (Stripe, Google, Twilio, Firebase, Apple, Google Play) remain the sole property of the Client.

Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep the terms of this SoW and any proprietary information shared during the project strictly confidential. Neither party shall disclose such information to any third party without prior written consent, except as required by law.

Change Control

Any request to add, remove, or modify deliverables beyond Section 3 must follow a formal process: written Change Request submitted, Developer provides impact assessment within 5 business days, both parties sign off in writing before work commences. Verbal agreements do not constitute approved changes.

Validity
This document is valid for 30 days from the date of issue. After this period, the scope and timelines are subject to review.
